01 · The Problem

Hospitality is missing
the moment.

01
Experiences are forgettable

Premium hospitality competes on food quality, service, and ambiance — but rarely creates a memory that outlasts the evening. Guests leave satisfied, not transformed. The social currency of a meal has collapsed into commodity.

02
SUNSETS is the world's most shared moment

Every day, millions photograph sunsets and share them globally. It is the single most universally resonant natural phenomenon — accessible to everyone, yet always unique. No hospitality brand has ever owned it.

03
Geography limits the experience

A Manhattan sunset happens once, at a fixed time, only for those present. The most iconic skylines in the world are inaccessible to most people most of the time. Great views are a privilege of location and timing.

04
Premium group experiences are underserved

The market wants private, curated, experience-first formats with high social capital. Corporate events, celebrations, and high-net-worth gatherings lack a dedicated luxury venue concept built entirely around them.

02 · The Concept

One venue.
Every sunset on Earth.

"Always sunset somewhere" — a premium rooftop experience where guests choose their skyline, not their table.

SUNSETS is a premium rooftop bar and restaurant concept built around one breakthrough idea: 4K sunset scenes from iconic cities worldwide — displayed through high-end LED installations within private, themed niches — intimate group booths, each transporting guests to a different global skyline.

As real sunsets roll across time zones, SUNSETS venues experience a continuous cascade of golden hours. The experience is always live, always unique, always worth photographing. Phase 1 uses exquisitely produced 4K loops. Phase 2 upgrades to live feeds as the network grows.

🌅
4K Scene Infrastructure
High-definition scenes from a 24+ city library. Cinematic 8K LED installations. Light in each niche changes as the real sun sets over that city.
🗺️
10 Themed Niches Per Venue
Each niche seats 8–10 guests privately. Guests book a city, not a table — creating identity and emotional connection to their experience.
🔄
Network Effects
Each new venue adds a live feed to every other. 24+ scene library rotates every 6–12 months. Regulars never see the same sunset twice.

Licensing Model

Hotels license the SUNSETS brand, concept, and technology. Upfront fee plus royalty. SUNSETS maintains brand control and IP. Hotel provides rooftop and operational infrastructure.

Asset-light · Scalable
🤝
Joint Venture

SUNSETS co-owns and co-operates with hotel partners. Shared capital investment and shared upside. Preferred for flagship locations where full brand expression is essential.

Control · Flagship
📊
Revenue Share

Zero upfront cost to hotel. SUNSETS takes a percentage of revenue generated. Lowest friction model for market entry. Hotel provides the rooftop; SUNSETS provides the concept.

07 · Risk & Mitigation

Eyes open.
Mitigations ready.

Key Risks
Concept ReplicationA well-funded hospitality group could copy the model once it's public and proven.
Technology ComplexityHigh-quality streaming across time zones in a hospitality setting is technically demanding and expensive to build.
Loop FatigueRegulars returning to the same scenes repeatedly could erode the novelty that drives repeat visits.
Execution at ScaleConsistent brand experience across multiple cities and operators requires tight operational standards.
Mitigations
IP Protection & SpeedTrademark brand assets immediately. Build brand equity and network effects fast — these become the real moat.
Phase 1 = Zero Streaming RiskLaunch with 4K loops. No live feed dependency. Upgrade to live as network grows and tech is proven.
24+ Scene Rotation SystemLibrary rotates every 6–12 months from 24+ city pool. Full-booth event mode fills dead hours at premium rates.
Licensing FrameworkDetailed brand standards, training, and operating manuals. Revenue share aligns partner incentives. Audits maintain quality.
