## Experience model
- Approximately 8–10 destination niches / booths in the pilot direction.
- Curated staggered time-zone sequencing is the preferred default room mode.
- A 24+ destination library supports rotation, events and future expansion.
- Phase 1 can launch with premium cinematic 4K/8K loops.
- Phase 2 can add selective live feeds and network effects.

## Open product decision
Guest scene selection vs Passport collectability remains intentionally unresolved:
- Keep a curated staggered sequence as the room default.
- Allow guest choice in limited/premium/event modes rather than unrestricted switching.
- Passport completion should depend on eligible booth/horizon experiences, active rotations, dates or venue visits, not merely selecting every scene in one sitting.
- Final rewards and economics require pilot validation.
