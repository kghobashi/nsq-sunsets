# SUNSETS — Brand & Website Story

## Brand
**SUNSETS** is a premium hospitality concept by Studio NSQ built around the transition from day into night.

**Selling slogan:** **WHERE EVERY HOUR IS GOLDEN.**

**Core philosophy:** *It’s always sunset somewhere.*

The philosophy explains the system. The slogan sells the feeling.

---

## The central idea
Few moments are photographed more instinctively than a sunset. Across beaches, rooftops, deserts, cities and mountains, people stop, turn toward the horizon and lift a phone.

SUNSETS takes that universally understood behavior and turns it into a hospitality ritual.

Guests do not simply book a table. They choose a horizon.

The physical rooftop, food, drink, service and architecture remain real. Technology extends the view beyond the city the guest is standing in, allowing one venue to move through different golden hours around the world.

The technology should disappear. The guest should remember the horizon, the people they were with, and how the evening felt.

---

## Guest promise
**Choose a horizon. Stay for the afterglow.**

A SUNSETS visit moves through four simple stages:

1. **Arrive** — enter a rooftop shaped by open sky, calm materials and changing light.
2. **Choose a Horizon** — select a destination atmosphere such as New York, Serengeti, Arizona Salt Flats or Tokyo.
3. **Golden Hour** — light, sound, scent, taste and service subtly align with the selected horizon.
4. **Afterglow** — one world moves into night as another approaches sunset. The venue continues evolving.

---

## Experience system
Each destination is a complete but restrained sensory environment.

### Vision
Architectural display surfaces and cinematic or live horizon content. Screens should read as windows, not televisions.

### Sound
Directional soundscapes that give each destination atmosphere without contaminating adjacent spaces.

### Scent
Low-intensity environmental cues: rain, cedar, salt air, dry grass, flowers, warm earth.

### Taste
Cocktails and selected dishes that reference each destination without turning the concept into a themed restaurant.

### Touch
Materials and furniture reinforce each atmosphere through texture, temperature and finish.

---

## Signature horizons

### New York
Metropolitan golden hour. Manhattan turning amber across glass and stone. Dark walnut, smoked glass, restrained metal, city texture and a Smoked Manhattan.

### Serengeti
Open plains at dusk. Copper, ochre and violet across a near-infinite horizon. Linen, stitched leather, teak, dry grass, acacia and warm wind.

### Arizona Salt Flats
A thin layer of water turns the landscape into a mirror. Sky above and below. Pale stone, glass, polished metal, mineral clarity and near-silence.

These are examples, not fixed permanent rooms. The destination library should evolve.

---

## The network
The long-term advantage is not one rooftop.

Every new SUNSETS location can become another real horizon available to the network. A Dubai guest could experience a live sunset from Tokyo, Cape Town or New York. A future New York venue could receive Dubai, Bali or Serengeti.

Each new location therefore increases the experiential value of the others.

**The network is the moat.**

---

## SUNSETS Passport
The loyalty layer turns repeat visits into collection.

Guests collect:
- horizons
- venues
- seasonal experiences
- special events

Passport status can unlock priority reservations, members-only events and cross-city benefits.

The program should reward discovery, not feel like a discount card.

---

## Hospitality positioning
SUNSETS is not:
- a nightclub
- a themed restaurant
- a screen installation
- a technology demo

SUNSETS is:
- a destination rooftop bar and restaurant
- a premium social ritual
- a globally connected hospitality brand
- an experience designed to be photographed and shared naturally

---

## Partner proposition
SUNSETS is designed to transform an existing premium rooftop into a destination within the destination.

Hotel and venue partners gain:
- non-guest traffic
- premium F&B positioning
- private-event potential
- longer dwell time
- built-in social content
- a concept that can refresh without rebuilding the venue

Potential commercial structures:
- licensing
- joint venture
- revenue share

## Visual identity
Display type: **POINA**, uppercase, horizon-E feature enabled.

Body type: **Montserrat Light 300**.

Core visual palette:
- Dusk navy / deep blue
- open-sky blue
- warm amber
- pale golden-hour glow
- restrained peach / afterglow
- cool white

The amber should feel like sunlight, not yellow branding.

Photography should feel cinematic, expansive and real. Avoid generic nightlife photography and over-saturated orange sunsets.

---

## Build principle
The website should feel close to the eventual customer-facing SUNSETS brand site, not like a presentation deck or internal concept hub.

The public site sells the experience.

Investor economics and detailed technology remain deeper supporting layers.
